# Konbini Vidéo Club Shelf

Every film named by guests of Konbini's **Vidéo Club** YouTube series, in one searchable page:
search and filter by film, director, guest, genre, year and sentiment, and jump to the exact
moment in the episode where the guest talks about it.

**Open it:** https://vitaminclaude.github.io/konbini-video-club/

- 177 episodes, 4,868 film mentions, 2,767 films
- Built from YouTube's own captions (often auto-generated), read and summarised in English.
  Some titles were identified from what the guest describes, so a few may be wrong.
- Comments are short English paraphrases, not transcripts.

Not affiliated with Konbini. All videos belong to their owners; links point to the original episodes on YouTube.
